20 dp circle fades in over the timestamp. Swiping up also dismisses.
Entrance
420 ms
Drops from -80 to 16 with a small overshoot. Scale grows from 0.9 to 1.
Hold
4.5 s
Hover pauses the timer. On leave it resumes with 2 s left.
Retract
260 ms
Cubic ease-in upward, quicker than the entrance, fading out as it leaves.
Swap
140 + 220 ms
With a queue, the pill collapses, crossfades its content, then expands again.
